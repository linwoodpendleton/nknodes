Self-hosting without the glue work

Running your own infrastructure usually means juggling a dozen tools: one for sites, one for certificates, one for backups, one for networking. The gaps between them are where the real work hides.

NodeKeeper removes that glue work. It is a self-hosted platform that puts website hosting, virtual machines, cloud desktops, Ceph distributed storage, and cross-site networking under a single management panel, so developers and IT teams stop maintaining the connections between separate tools. Per-site system users and resource limits keep multi-tenant hosting clean, and automated Ceph deployment with snapshots and HA tooling supports private clouds on your own hardware.
