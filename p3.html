A complete toolkit for self-hosted infrastructure

Choosing a self-hosted platform means deciding what you want to stop fixing by hand. The list is long: certificate renewals, firewall rules, backup verification, cross-site latency, desktop provisioning.

NodeKeeper covers that whole list in one place. It is self-hosted infrastructure management: websites, virtual machines, cloud desktops, distributed storage, and networking, all from a single panel. Website hosting includes the full site and database lifecycle with SSL, WAF, backups, and audit trails. When you grow beyond one box, Ceph deployment, snapshots, and HA management keep the storage layer reliable. Office environments get Windows cloud desktops and PXE diskless boot, centralized and networked across sites.
